This report presents the practical execution of advanced Excel functionalities for Week 2: Advanced Excel Functionality and Formula Execution, using a hypothetical business scenario for FreshKart, a multi-store grocery retail chain. The document introduces the problem statement of manual, error-prone reporting and defines a simulated environment with 15,000 transactions, product and store masters. It outlines a 6-step execution strategy from scenario design to verification. A major section explains advanced functions in depth, including dynamic array formulas FILTER, SORT, UNIQUE, SEQUENCE and SUMPRODUCT, nested logical statements IF, IFS and SWITCH, and lookup formulas XLOOKUP, INDEX+MATCH and XMATCH with syntax and advantages. Ten complex business scenarios are solved step-by-step, such as tiered discount calculation with IFS, safe category lookup with XLOOKUP, gross profit computation, dynamic top-5 products with SORT and UNIQUE, high-value transaction extraction with FILTER, monthly regional sales vs target with SUMIFS and EOMONTH, performance rating with nested IF-AND, and weighted average discount with SUMPRODUCT. Verification techniques using Evaluate Formula, Trace Precedents, F9 evaluation, IFERROR testing and PivotTable cross-validation are detailed. Outcomes, potential pitfalls like #SPILL! and #N/A cascades, troubleshooting framework, proposed enhancements with LET, LAMBDA and Power Query, and best practices are discussed to ensure a robust, automated, analysis-ready workbook.
